# SwiftSign launch video — voiceover script (draft v1)

~115 words, ~80s at launch-post pace. Developer-native, concrete, no hype.
Banned words avoided (agents, leverage, robust, synergy, innovative). No em dashes.

| Beat | Time | VO |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00-0:05 | Sending a contract still means leaving your work. A browser. A PDF upload. Someone else's dashboard. |
| Terminal hero | 0:05-0:28 | SwiftSign doesn't. Install it, and from the same terminal you already live in, send a real, legally binding contract. It reads the document, finds the signature anchors, and emails your client. |
| Transition | 0:28-0:33 | They open it on their phone. |
| Signer | 0:33-0:52 | And sign in seconds. No account. No app. |
| Seal + audit | 0:52-1:07 | The moment they finish, SwiftSign seals the PDF, writes a full ESIGN, UETA, and PIPEDA audit trail, and fires the signed event straight back to your terminal. |
| Positioning | 1:07-1:17 | Contracts as code. Send, track, and seal them from where you already work. |
| CTA | 1:17-1:22 | SwiftSign. npm install swiftsign. |

## Notes
- Voice option A: ElevenLabs (calm, technical, mid-pace male or neutral). Option B: Shahdad records it (more authentic for a founder launch).
- Music: minimal, building, electronic-but-restrained (Artlist/Epidemic) or Suno. Sits under VO, lifts at the seal beat.
- The "fires the signed event back to your terminal" line is the loop-closer: visually, the opener terminal shows the `signed` webhook event arriving.
